# CampusLoop Opportunities — Product Blueprint

## 1. Vision & Core Mission
**CampusLoop Opportunities** is an authoritative, high-density discovery platform for collegiate opportunities: internships, hackathons, scholarships, research fellowships, coding competitions, and student programs. Part of the **CampusLoop Labs** family (`https://campusloop.space`).

## 2. Target Audience & User Personas
- **Engineering & Science Undergrads**: High scannability, urgent deadline alerts, direct stipend/prize visibility, fast filtering by remote/hybrid/in-person.
- **Student Researchers & Scholars**: Looking for institutional grants, GSoC, AICTE, and university fellowships.
- **Autonomous AI Publishing Agents**: Publishing and managing opportunities via programmatic MCP tools over streamable HTTP.

## 3. Experience Pillars
1. **Zero Noise, 100% Verified**: No expired spam, misleading unpaid jobs masquerading as internships, or broken redirect links.
2. **Instant Scannability**: High information density, concise metadata chips, urgency pills, and immediate multi-platform sharing (WhatsApp college groups, Telegram, LinkedIn, X).
3. **Accessibility & Dark Mode**: Seamless reading in daytime library environments and late-night coding sessions with high-contrast, eye-friendly themes.
4. **Machine & Human Interoperability**: Human-first web interface paired with an official Model Context Protocol (MCP) server for autonomous agents.
